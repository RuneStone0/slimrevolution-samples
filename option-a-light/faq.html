Frequently asked questions

General

Do I need to be in shape to start?
No. Every program begins with a body-composition scan and is built around your current fitness level — beginners and post-partum clients are welcome.
Is this weight-loss medication?
No. We don't offer GLP-1s, Ozempic, or peptides. We deliver results through measured training, technology, and coaching.
Do you work with men?
Yes — our studio is built for women and men, with programs tailored to each person's goals.

VacuTherm

What is the VacuTherm Treadmill?
A low-impact treadmill that combines vacuum pressure, full-spectrum infrared, and red light in one 30-minute session. Vendor-reported to burn up to 2–3× more calories than a regular treadmill. Results vary by individual.
Does it hurt?
No. Most clients describe it as a warm, gentle workout — you walk or lightly jog while the intensity adjusts to you.

Lymph Rollers

What is a Lymph Roller session?
A 30-minute mechanical lymphatic massage with infrared — it supports drainage, recovery, and firming. Ideal right after a VacuTherm workout.

EMSculpt & Coaching

How does EMSculpt feel?
Intense muscle contractions, comparable to doing thousands of crunches or squats in 30 minutes — without the joint strain.
Are your coaches certified?
Yes — our coaching is ISSA-certified, and every plan is personalized to your scan and goals.

Booking & visits

How do I book a session?
Book online from the Book Online page, or call the studio on (385) 616-2266. Members can also book, reschedule and cancel from the Bookings page in the member area.
What is your cancellation policy?
Change or cancel up to 12 hours before your session at no cost. Inside 12 hours the session is charged, because the slot can no longer be offered to anyone else.
Do I need to sign a waiver?
Yes — one waiver covers you for all sessions. It takes a couple of minutes and can be signed from your account before your first visit.
What should I bring, and where do I park?
Wear comfortable clothes and bring water. We are at 166 Pioneer Crossing, Ste 105, Saratoga Springs — free parking is directly outside, and towels are provided.

Membership & billing

Can I pause my membership?
Yes. Memberships can be paused once a year for up to four weeks. Ask at the front desk or send a message from Settings; your scan history and member rate are kept.
Which membership should I choose?
It depends how often you want to be in. Revitalizing Glow suits two to three visits a week, Endless Energy adds priority booking, and Target Transformation adds weekly EMSculpt and the monthly coaching check-in. Start with the $50 consultation and we will recommend one.

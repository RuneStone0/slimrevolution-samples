What is VacuTherm?

The VacuTherm Treadmill is a premium cardio machine that wraps three therapies into a single 30-minute session.

🌀

Vacuum (negative pressure)

A fitted neoprene skirt creates gentle suction around your lower body while you walk — increasing blood flow to the abdomen, thighs, and buttocks as you move.

🔥

Full-spectrum infrared

Six Philips infrared lamps warm the body to support circulation and recovery during your workout.

🔴

Red light therapy (650–670 nm)

Added to support collagen and skin, so the session works on firmness as much as fitness.

You control the intensity — vacuum level, infrared power, speed, and incline all adjust to your fitness level and goals.

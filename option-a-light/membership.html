FAQ

Membership FAQ

What's included in the $300/mo membership?
Up to 3 VacuTherm & Lymph sessions per week, monthly body-composition scans, and coaching. Higher tiers add unlimited sessions and EMSculpt.
How is this different from a regular gym membership?
You get a measured plan, not just access to equipment. Every month we scan, chart, and adjust your program with a certified coach.
Do I have to commit long-term?
Monthly plans are month-to-month; annual plans offer a discounted rate if you're ready to commit.
What is a body-composition scan?
An 8-point bioimpedance analysis that measures 21 metrics — body fat, muscle mass, visceral fat, bone density, and metabolic age — in about 15 minutes.
Can I try before I join?
Yes — book a $50 Weight Loss Consultation or a single VacuTherm session ($45) first, and we'll help you choose the right plan.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

General

Do I need to be in shape to start?
No. Every program begins with a body-composition scan and is built around your current fitness level — beginners and post-partum clients are welcome.
Is this weight-loss medication?
No. We don't offer GLP-1s, Ozempic, or peptides. We deliver results through measured training, technology, and coaching.
Do you work with men?
Yes — our studio is built for women and men, with programs tailored to each person's goals.

VacuTherm

What is the VacuTherm Treadmill?
A low-impact treadmill that combines vacuum pressure, full-spectrum infrared, and red light in one 30-minute session. Vendor-reported to burn up to 2–3× more calories than a regular treadmill. Results vary by individual.
Does it hurt?
No. Most clients describe it as a warm, gentle workout — you walk or lightly jog while the intensity adjusts to you.

Lymph Rollers

What is a Lymph Roller session?
A 30-minute mechanical lymphatic massage with infrared — it supports drainage, recovery, and firming. Ideal right after a VacuTherm workout.

EMSculpt & Coaching

How does EMSculpt feel?
Intense muscle contractions, comparable to doing thousands of crunches or squats in 30 minutes — without the joint strain.
Are your coaches certified?
Yes — our coaching is ISSA-certified, and every plan is personalized to your scan and goals.
